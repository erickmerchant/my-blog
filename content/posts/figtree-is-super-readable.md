I recently was trying to find a new font to use, and came across
[Figtree](https://www.erikdkennedy.com/projects/figtree.html) by Erik Kennedy.
It's available on Google Fonts. I'm not a font expert at all, so I can not say
much about it technically, but I can just say I'm so taken with it. It's so
readable and friendly. I feel like I may stick with this one.

I recently made a tool
[@erickmerchant/fonts](https://github.com/erickmerchant/fonts) available on JSR,
for quickly grabbing a font from Google, subset it, and writing the CSS. It
allows me to switch fonts quickly, but also update them if needed. Most
importantly it allows a new project to quickly add a font.
